# Appendix C — Future Enhancements (Out of Current Scope)

The following items are explicitly out of scope for the initial release and are documented here for planning purposes only; they are not part of the requirements baseline in Sections 3–8.

## C.1 Editing After Upload — Extended Policy

A formal correction/versioning process beyond the short post-upload edit window described in [FR-REC-3–4](03-system-features/3.8-record-actions.md) (e.g., full version history of edits) is deferred to a future phase.

## C.2 Approval Workflow

A future enhancement is to require review and approval before a CM document becomes visible in the repository, rather than publishing immediately on upload:

![Proposed future approval workflow](diagrams/approval.png)

*Figure 4 — Proposed future approval workflow (Draft → Pending Review → Approved → Published)*

This workflow would add process rigor and a second set of eyes on sensitive documentation, at the cost of additional latency between an incident and the CM being visible in the system. It should be evaluated against HR's existing sign-off process before being added to a future requirements baseline.

## C.3 Other Enhancements

- Configurable notification/reminder emails ahead of a CM's expiry date
- Bulk export of reports to Excel/CSV for offline HR analysis
- E-signature capture for employee acknowledgment of a CM at the point of issuance
- Full-text search within uploaded CM letters, not just metadata
